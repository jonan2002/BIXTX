1. **Icon**: 📱 (Mobile phone emoji)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see the QR Code Manager tab"

**Check:**
1. ✅ Are you logged in as Admin?
2. ✅ Do you have QR code permissions?
3. ✅ Is your browser up to date?
4. ✅ Try refreshing the page (F5)

### "I see the tab but it's grayed out"

**Solution:**
- You may not have permissions
- Contact your administrator
- Check your user role

### "The tab exists but clicking does nothing"

**Solution:**
1. Clear browser cache
2. Try different browser
3. Check JavaScript is enabled
4. Contact support
